### GENERAL_UNKNOWN {#general_unknown}

> An unknown error occurred.

---

### GENERAL_TIMEOUT {#general_timeout}

> A timeout occurred.

---

### GENERAL_INVALID_MANAGER {#general_invalid_manager}

> An invalid manager was provided.

---

### MANAGER_INIT_FAILED {#manager_init_failed}

> Failed to initialize the manager.

---

### MANAGER_INVALID_CONFIG {#manager_invalid_config}

> The manager configuration is invalid or incomplete.

---

### MANAGER_SHUTDOWN_FAILED {#manager_shutdown_failed}

> The manager failed to shut down properly.

---

### MANAGER_NO_NODES {#manager_no_nodes}

> No Lavalink nodes were configured or available.

---

### MANAGER_NODE_NOT_FOUND {#manager_node_not_found}

> The requested Lavalink node could not be found.

---

### MANAGER_SEARCH_FAILED {#manager_search_failed}

> The track search operation failed.

---

### MANAGER_CLEANUP_INACTIVE_PLAYERS_FAILED {#manager_cleanup_inactive_players_failed}

> Failed to clean up inactive players.

---


### NODE_INVALID_CONFIG {#node_invalid_config}

---

### NODE_CONNECT_FAILED {#node_connect_failed}

> Failed to establish a connection to the Lavalink node.

---

### NODE_RECONNECT_FAILED {#node_reconnect_failed}

> Failed to reconnect to the Lavalink node after disconnection.

---

### NODE_DISCONNECTED {#node_disconnected}

> The Lavalink node was unexpectedly disconnected.

---

### NODE_PROTOCOL_ERROR {#node_protocol_error}

---

### NODE_SESSION_IDS_LOAD_FAILED {#node_session_ids_load_failed}

---

###  NODE_SESSION_IDS_UPDATE_FAILED {#node_session_ids_update_failed}

---

### PLAYER_INVALID_CONFIG {#player_invalid_config}

---

### PLAYER_STATE_INVALID {#player_state_invalid}

---

---

### PLAYER_PREVIOUS_EMPTY {#player_previous_empty}

> There are no previous tracks to play.

---

---

### PLAYER_INVALID_VOLUME {#player_invalid_volume}

---

### PLAYER_INVALID_REPEAT {#player_invalid_repeat}

---

---
